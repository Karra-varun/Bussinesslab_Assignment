Why This Project: 10x to 20x Developer Leverage

Software that cost a client $2,000 two years ago now sells for about $200 (a 90% drop in price). Because clients will not pay more than the highest tier of Cursor or Claude, technical companies must produce 10 to 20 times more work to pay the same salaries. Code is now the fast part. The slow part is everything around it: knowing the product inside out, answering every customer email quickly and correctly, and turning long client meetings into a clear scope of work. The AI Tech Consultant connects these three bottlenecks into one unified, self-evolving system.
